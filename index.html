Privacy Policy

At Donuts, we value your privacy and are committed to protecting the personal information you share with us when ordering sweet treats or reaching out through our site.

1. Information We Collect

  • Contact Inquiries: Your name, email address, phone number, and mailing address when submitting our contact form.
  • Orders: Delivery address, phone number, name, selected items, and chosen payment method when placing an order.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To process, fulfill, and deliver your donut orders to your door.
  • To send order confirmation notifications via email.
  • To respond to support inquiries submitted via the contact form.

3. Data Protection & Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties. Customer details are strictly used to complete transactions and manage local cart preferences.
